# Dashboard and Quick Add

## Dashboard purpose

The dashboard answers three questions immediately: What happened today? What needs attention? What can I do next?

## Layout

### Header

- Greeting and business date.
- Global search by article number, product, invoice, customer, supplier, or phone.
- Prominent `+ Quick Add` button.
- Notifications and user menu.

### Summary strip

- Today's sales.
- Today's payments received.
- Today's expenses.
- Net cash movement.

### Owner/manager overview

- Customer dues.
- Supplier payables.
- Inventory value.
- Estimated monthly gross profit.

### Attention queue

- Low/out-of-stock products.
- Overdue customer dues.
- Supplier payments due.
- Pending or late deliveries.
- Draft documents awaiting action.

### Activity

- Recent sales and payments.
- Seven-day sales trend.
- Best-selling categories/products.

## Quick Add contents

The menu opens from the header or `Ctrl+Shift+A` and groups actions by frequency:

**Sell:** New Sale, New Quotation, Receive Customer Payment, Schedule Delivery.

**Stock:** Add Product, Add Furniture Set, Record Purchase, Adjust Stock, Transfer Stock.

**People and money:** Add Customer, Add Supplier, Pay Supplier, Add Expense.

**After-sale:** Record Return, Record Damage.

## Behavior

- Searchable by action name and synonym.
- Keyboard navigable; first suitable result selected.
- Role-aware: unauthorized actions do not appear.
- User may pin up to six actions as dashboard shortcut tiles.
- Defaults: New Sale, Add Product, Receive Customer Payment, Record Purchase, Add Expense, Schedule Delivery.
- Opening an action uses a focused modal for short tasks and a full page for multi-line documents.
- Successful completion refreshes only affected dashboard data.

## Quick forms

Quick Add may create a simple customer, supplier, expense, or payment inline. Sales, purchases, products with images, bundles, returns, and stock transfers use full screens to prevent mistakes.

## Privacy and roles

Salespeople may see operational counts but not total profit, inventory cost, or supplier balances unless permitted. Dashboard data comes from one role-filtered summary command to avoid leaking hidden figures.

## Empty state

On a fresh installation, show a setup checklist: Shop Profile, First Category, First Product, Opening Stock, First Customer/Supplier, Backup Location. The Quick Add button remains primary.
