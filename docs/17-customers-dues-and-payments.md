# Customers, Dues, Advances, and Payments

## Customer profile

Name, phone, alternate phone, address, notes, optional credit limit, active status, total purchases, current due, available advance, last sale, and last payment.

## Customer timeline

A single chronological view shows quotations, invoices, payments, advances, returns, refunds, deliveries, and account adjustments. Each entry links to its source document.

## Ledger convention

Use a documented debit/credit convention consistently. A posted credit sale increases amount owed. Payment, approved credit note, or advance allocation reduces it. The current balance is derived from valid ledger entries.

## Receiving payment

Quick Add > Receive Customer Payment:

1. Find customer by name or phone.
2. Show total due, advance, and open invoices.
3. Enter amount, date, method, account, reference, and note.
4. Allocate automatically oldest-first or manually.
5. Preview remaining balance.
6. Confirm and print/download receipt.

## Advances

A payment not fully allocated to invoices remains customer advance. Advance can fund a later sale only with explicit allocation. The system never shows the same amount as both received payment and outstanding due reduction twice.

## Credit control

- Optional customer credit limit and due date.
- Warn when a new sale exceeds limit or an overdue balance exists.
- Owner can require permission to override.
- Credit limit is not a guarantee and does not replace current balance checks.

## Payment correction

Payments are not edited after posting. A permitted void creates reversal ledger/cash entries and records a reason. Re-enter the correct payment as a new transaction.

## Statements

Customer statement includes opening balance, dated transactions, references, debits, credits, running balance, and closing balance. Filters: date range, branch/location if introduced, and transaction type. Output: screen, PDF, and CSV.

## Due reminders

The system lists due today, overdue, due soon, and high-balance accounts. Version 1 supports copyable message text and printable statements. Automated WhatsApp sending remains optional future integration.

## Privacy

Phone/address exports and statements require permission. Search screens reveal only the minimum customer information needed for the current task.
